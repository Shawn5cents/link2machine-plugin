## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one finished square app icon for Link2Machine, a public ChatGPT plugin that connects chat to a user's computer.
Input image: the supplied icon is a brand reference for the mint / indigo / near-black palette and the idea of a connection to a machine. Redesign its composition into a stronger, more memorable app mark.
Create one premium icon, not a presentation board or a mockup: two bold interlocking rounded rectangular links form one clean diagonal connection. Integrate a minimal terminal-screen shape into the lower-right link, with a crisp simple >_ prompt cutout inside it. It should instantly suggest "link to computer" with a unified geometric silhouette. Thick clean forms, spacious negative space, optical balance, vivid mint cyan transitioning into electric indigo. Restrained sculptural depth and a slight luminous rim give it energy; keep the shape clear and uncluttered.
Backdrop: an almost-black rounded-square tile (#05070B), with truly transparent pixels outside its rounded corners. Center the mark, generously sized but comfortably inset, with consistent safe margins for an app-store icon.
Color palette: #6DF0D6 mint, #6683FF indigo, a little cool pale-blue highlight. Match the established brand.
Constraints: exactly one icon, square front-facing composition, no words, no app name, no watermark, no badge, no background objects, no tiny circuitry, no fine lines. The only glyph is the terminal prompt >_. Must look striking at 512 pixels and stay legible at 48 pixels. Keep the background tile dark and preserve actual alpha outside the corners.
